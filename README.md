# Tailoes – Men, Women, Kids & Pets Tailoring and Sustainable Fashion Brand in India

Tailoes is a professional tailoring and sustainable fashion transformation service in India offering custom stitching, alteration, refitting, restyling, repurposing, embroidery, and wardrobe upgrade solutions for men, women, kids, and pets.

---

## Sustainable Fashion Mission

Tailoes focuses on reducing textile waste by transforming old garments into modern wearable outfits through expert tailoring, redesign, and clothing transformation services.
