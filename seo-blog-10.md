## What Is a Doorstep Women's Tailoring Consultation?

A doorstep women's tailoring consultation at Tailoes means
a stylist visits your home, takes your exact measurements,
understands your garment requirements and delivers the
finished outfit at your door.

No boutique visit. No multiple trips. No vague brief
explained to a stranger who may not remember it next week.

---

## The 5-Step Consultation Process

### Step 1 — WhatsApp Booking
Contact Tailoes via WhatsApp to confirm your service
requirement and book a consultation slot.

### Step 2 — Stylist Home Visit
A Tailoes stylist arrives at your home at the confirmed
time. Available across Bangalore — Indiranagar,
Koramangala, HSR Layout, Whitefield, Jayanagar
and surrounding areas.

### Step 3 — Measurements and Garment Brief
The stylist takes complete measurements and documents
the full garment brief — occasion, silhouette, fabric
preference and finishing details.

### Step 4 — Stitching and Quality Check
Your garment is stitched at the Tailoes studio and
goes through a quality check before dispatch.

### Step 5 — Delivery to Your Door
The finished outfit is delivered to your home.
The entire process requires zero visits from your side.
