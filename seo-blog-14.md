A practical guide to ordering custom-stitched clothing for dogs and cats online

## How to Measure (3 inputs)
| Measurement   | Where                                  |
|---------------|----------------------------------------|
| Neck girth    | Base of neck, where a collar sits      |
| Chest girth   | Widest point behind front legs (key)   |
| Back length   | Neck base → tail base (exclude tail)   |

Standard S/M/L sizing ignores breed shape. Custom is stitched to your pet's
actual measurements — better fit, better comfort.
