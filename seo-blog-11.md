## Why Fabric Selection Matters for Bangalore Men

Bangalore's climate varies significantly across the year —
humid summers, moderate monsoons and genuinely cold winter
nights from October to February. Formal occasions span
outdoor weddings, air-conditioned boardrooms, festive
celebrations and everyday professional environments.

Each occasion and season requires a different fabric. Getting
this wrong means wearing the right silhouette in the wrong
material — visually correct but physically uncomfortable.

---

## Fabric Guide by Occasion

### Everyday Formal and Office Wear
**Best fabric:** Cotton and cotton blends
**Why:** Breathable, non-heat-trapping, comfortable through
long office days. Works year-round in Bangalore's climate.
**Best for:** Formal shirts, casual suits, everyday kurtas

### Outdoor Events and Summer Weddings
**Best fabric:** Linen and linen blends
**Why:** Best breathability of any suiting fabric. Essential
for outdoor events where temperatures are high.
**Best for:** Summer wedding suits, outdoor ceremony wear

### Winter Events and Indoor Formal Occasions
**Best fabric:** Wool and wool blends
**Why:** Correct drape and structure for premium suiting.
Bangalore winters from October to February justify light
wool for formal events.
**Best for:** Two-piece suits, formal occasion jackets

### Wedding Sherwanis and Festive Ethnic Wear
**Best fabric:** Silk and silk blends
**Why:** Correct weight and drape for traditional formal
garments. Gives the right finish for wedding occasion wear.
**Best for:** Sherwanis, festive kurtas, ceremonial garments
