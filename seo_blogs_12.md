## Why Sizing Matters More for Indian Dogs

Standard S/M/L pet accessory sizing is built for Western breed
proportions. Indian dogs — Labradors, Beagles, Indie dogs and
Pomeranians — have different neck circumferences, coat types
and comfort needs. Custom measurement solves this completely.

## How to Measure Your Dog's Neck

1. Use a soft measuring tape
2. Wrap around the widest part of the neck
3. Add 2cm for comfort
4. Share this measurement when ordering from Tailoes
